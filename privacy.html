Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The protocol is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, to view their own health data. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads Google Health data (activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, profile and settings) through read-only scopes. It never writes to Google Health.

How data is used and stored

Data is downloaded to the owner's own computer and shown in a dashboard that runs only on that computer (localhost). OAuth credentials are kept in the operating system's keyring.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. No data is sent anywhere other than the owner's own computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
